================================================================================
PROJECT SUMMARY
================================================================================

Arka is a cross-platform AI terminal agent (PyPI: arka-agent, MIT).
Users ask in plain language; Arka routes to local skills, then LLM fallback.
Canonical docs: https://arka-agent.mintlify.site — fetch /llms.txt, then one page.
