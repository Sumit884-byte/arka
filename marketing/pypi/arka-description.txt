========================================================================
LONG
========================================================================
Arka upgrades your terminal into a skillful AI agent. You write natural
language; Arka matches it with 120+ symbolic routing rules when possible,
then dispatches to 70+ local skills that run on your machine. When an LLM
is required, a failover chain covers Gemini, Groq, Ollama, OpenRouter,
and more. Security gates are on by default. Use it from the CLI, voice,
or as an MCP server in Cursor and other IDEs.
